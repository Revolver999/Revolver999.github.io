Twelvo Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

In short: everything you type into Twelvo stays on your device. Twelvo has no accounts and no server, so the developer never sees your rota, pay, money, lists or notes. The free version shows adverts from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to do so. Twelvo Pro shows no adverts.

Who we are

Twelvo is a personal organiser for shift workers, published by Francesco Mascia ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, contact us at terrajar.support@gmail.com.

What you put into Twelvo

Twelvo lets you record shifts, pay details, income, bills and spending, what you own and owe, pension details, trips, lists, notes and habits. All of this is saved only in the app's storage on your own device.

  • It is not sent to us or to anyone else. We have no copy and cannot read, recover or delete it for you.
  • You do not create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • If you use Back up, Twelvo makes a file that you choose where to save or share. That file is in your hands, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • Deleting the app, or using the reset option in Settings, deletes your data from the device. Your device's own backup (for example iCloud Backup) may hold a copy under your Apple account, which Apple controls and we cannot see.

Microphone and voice notes

If you use the microphone to dictate a note, Twelvo asks for permission first. Your speech is turned into text by the speech recognition service built into your device, which is provided by Apple and may send audio to Apple to do this. Twelvo keeps only the resulting text, on your device. We do not receive or store any audio. You can refuse or withdraw this permission in your device settings and still use the rest of the app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled on your device. They are not sent from a server.

Adverts in the free version

The free version of Twelvo shows adverts supplied by Google AdMob. To show and measure adverts, and to prevent fraud, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking;
  • your IP address, which gives an approximate location such as country or city;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • how you interact with adverts, and diagnostic data such as crash and performance information from the advertising software.

Nothing you type into Twelvo is shared with Google or any advertiser.

The first time adverts would be shown, your device asks whether to allow Twelvo to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you still see adverts but they are not personalised using the advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking. Where the law requires it, you are also asked for consent before personalised adverts are shown, and you can change that choice in Twelvo's Settings.

Google explains how it uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and its privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Twelvo Pro and purchases

Twelvo Pro is bought through Apple's in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment. We never see your card or bank details. Apple tells the app whether a purchase is active, and may give us summary sales information that does not identify you. Twelvo Pro shows no adverts, so the advertising data described above is not collected while Pro is active.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not run our own analytics or tracking in the app.
  • We do not connect to your bank, employer, NHS or pension accounts. Any figures in the app are ones you typed in.

Children

Twelvo is made for working adults. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, data protection law gives you rights over your personal data, including to access it, correct it, delete it and object to its use. Because your Twelvo content is held only on your device, you exercise these rights over it directly in the app. For the advertising data described above, Google is the party that holds it, and you can use the controls above or Google's own tools at adssettings.google.com. You can also contact us, and you have the right to complain to your data protection authority, which in the UK is the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Not financial advice

Pay, tax, pension and retirement figures in Twelvo are estimates based on what you enter and on published rates. They are for personal planning and are not financial advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. If the change is significant, it will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.
